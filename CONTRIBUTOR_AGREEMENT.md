# Contributor copyright assignment and license agreement

**Template for legal review before accepting outside contributions.** This is a
copyright assignment to Imagination Frontier, LLC, not the Apache Software
Foundation's contributor license agreement. A checkbox or Git commit alone is
not the signature process described below.

## Plain-language explanation

If you sign this agreement, you transfer ownership of the copyright you own in
your submitted contributions to **Imagination Frontier, LLC** (the Company).
The Company can maintain, publish, change and license those contributions,
including under commercial or other licenses. You keep a broad license to use
your own contributions. This agreement does not give the Company ownership of
unrelated work, third-party material, or your patents. It grants a limited patent
license needed to use your contributions.

The project's published code remains available under Apache-2.0. This agreement
does not take away rights already granted to recipients under that license.

## Agreement

1. **Definitions.** “You” means the individual or legal entity identified in the
   signature block. “Contribution” means original code, documentation, artwork or
   other copyrightable material that You own and intentionally submit for
   inclusion in the refresh-oss project after signing this agreement, together
   with any earlier contributions specifically identified in the signature block.
   It excludes submissions clearly marked “Not a Contribution” and third-party
   or pre-existing material that You identify as excluded when submitting it.
   Feedback and ordinary bug reports are not assigned unless expressly submitted
   as Contributions under this agreement.

2. **Copyright assignment.** In consideration of the mutual promises in this
   agreement, including the license back below, You hereby assign to the Company
   all Your worldwide right, title and interest in the copyright in each
   Contribution, including rights to reproduce, modify, distribute, publicly
   perform, publicly display and sublicense it, for the full duration of those
   rights and any extensions or renewals. For future Contributions, the assignment
   takes effect on submission to the extent permitted by law. Where an assignment
   cannot take effect automatically, You agree to execute a confirmatory assignment.

3. **Fallback license and moral rights.** To the extent a copyright cannot be
   assigned, You grant the Company an exclusive, worldwide, perpetual,
   irrevocable, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable license to exercise
   those rights, subject to the license back below. To the extent allowed by law,
   You waive and agree not to assert moral rights in Contributions against the
   Company or its licensees. Where waiver is unavailable, You consent to the
   Company's and its licensees' exercise of the rights granted here to the extent
   that consent is legally effective.

4. **License back to You.** The Company grants You a perpetual, worldwide,
   non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable
   license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, publicly perform, publicly
   display and otherwise exploit Your Contributions for any purpose. This does
   not grant rights to other contributors' work, Company trademarks or confidential
   information.

5. **Patent license.** You grant the Company and recipients of the project a
   perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable patent license
   to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import and otherwise transfer
   Your Contributions, alone or in combination with the project to which You
   submitted them. This grant applies only to patent claims You can license that
   are necessarily infringed by Your Contributions alone or by that combination.
   It does not assign ownership of any patent. If a recipient institutes patent
   litigation alleging that Your Contribution or the project constitutes direct
   or contributory patent infringement, the patent licenses You granted to that
   recipient under this agreement for that Contribution terminate on filing.

6. **Authority and disclosure.** You represent that You own the rights You assign
   or are authorized to assign them; that any employer or other rights holder
   whose permission is required has authorized this agreement; and that, to Your
   knowledge, Your Contributions do not infringe others' rights. You will clearly
   identify third-party material, its source and license, and any restrictions or
   excluded material with each submission. Do not submit secrets or material You
   are not authorized to publish. An individual signature does not transfer an
   employer's rights without the employer's authorization.

7. **No obligation to accept or support.** The Company need not accept or publish
   any Contribution. Except for the express representations above, Contributions
   are provided “AS IS,” without warranties of any kind. You have no obligation
   to provide support, updates or future Contributions.

8. **Further documents and changes.** You will reasonably cooperate in signing
   documents needed to confirm the assignment, at the Company's expense. Changes
   to this agreement must be in a writing signed by You and the Company. Invalidity
   of one provision does not invalidate the remaining provisions to the extent
   they can operate lawfully.

## Maintainer note

Have counsel review this template, the consideration, jurisdiction-specific rules,
employer authority and the signing/record-retention process before relying on it.
A normal Apache contributor license does **not** transfer copyright ownership.
For background, see the [Apache licensing guidance](https://apache.org/legal/apply-license.html)
and [U.S. Copyright Act § 204](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap2.html),
which addresses signed writings for copyright transfers.
